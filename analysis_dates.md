# Comparative Analysis: Start Dates

## 1. Early 1942 (Spring)
*   **Scenario:** Before Stalingrad, before El Alamein, during "Second Happy Time" in Atlantic.
*   **Impact:** Germany avoids all major strategic blunders. Tech implementation has 2+ years to mature before Western invasion.
*   **Result:** **Likely German Victory.** The "Time Traveler" makes them invincible.
*   **Uncertainty:** Low.

## 2. Late 1944 (Autumn)
*   **Scenario:** Allies on the Rhine, Soviets in Poland. Industry shattered.
*   **Impact:** Tech arrives too late to be built. Intelligence helps win local battles (e.g., Market Garden, Bulge), but attrition kills the Reich.
*   **Result:** **Likely German Defeat.** (Historical "Wonder Weapon" scenario).
*   **Uncertainty:** Low.

## 3. Early 1941 (Pre-Barbarossa)
*   **Scenario:** Germany controls Europe, unbloodied.
*   **Impact:** Perfect strategic planning for Russia. US entry managed/delayed.
*   **Result:** **Guaranteed German Hegemony.**
*   **Uncertainty:** Zero.

## 4. June 20, 1943 (The "Pivot")
*   **Scenario:** The tide has *just* turned. Stalingrad is lost, North Africa is lost, U-boats are beaten. But the Wehrmacht is still a potent cohesive force.
*   **Impact:**
    *   **Intelligence:** Stops the bleeding immediately (Kursk, Ultra).
    *   **Tech:** Arrives *during* the crisis. Creates a race between "Allied Momentum" and "German Innovation".
*   **Result:** **Maximum Uncertainty.**
    *   Germany is in a deep hole, but has been given a ladder. Can they climb out before the hole collapses?

## Conclusion
The provided date of **June 20, 1943** is analytically superior for the goal of a "50/50 Outcome". No adjustment is recommended.
